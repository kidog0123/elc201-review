📖 Công thức & Tóm tắt ELC201

1. Op-Amp (Operational Amplifier)

  • Inverting Amplifier: $A_v = - \frac{R_f}{R_1}$
  • Non-inverting Amplifier: $A_v = 1 + \frac{R_f}{R_1}$
  • Voltage Follower (Buffer): $A_v = 1$, $Z_{in} \approx \infty$, $Z_{out} \approx 0$
  • Slew Rate: $SR = \frac{\Delta V_{out}}{\Delta t}$ (741 Op-Amp: $SR \approx 0.5\text{ V/}\mu\text{s}$)

2. JFET & BJT

  • Shockley's Equation: $I_D = I_{DSS} \left(1 - \frac{V_{GS}}{V_P}\right)^2$
  • Transconductance: $g_m = \frac{\Delta I_D}{\Delta V_{GS}} = g_{m0} \left(1 - \frac{V_{GS}}{V_P}\right)$
  • Common Emitter: Phase shift $180^\circ$
  • Common Collector (Emitter Follower): $A_v \approx 1$, High $Z_{in}$

3. Bộ dao động & Bộ định thời (555 Timer)

  • Astable 555: $f = \frac{1.44}{(R_A + 2R_B)C}$
  • Monostable 555: $T = 1.1 RC$
  • Wien Bridge Oscillator: $f_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}$, feedback fraction $= 1/3$
